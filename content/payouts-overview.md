# Payouts Overview

A payout is an instruction to send funds from your available account balance to a beneficiary through a supported destination and payout rail.

> Before creating a payout, make sure your account has sufficient available balance in the source currency.
>
> Funding and payouts are separate processes. Once funds are available in your account balance, they can be used to create payouts.

---

## What a payout represents

A payout can be used for several business cases:

- Remittances
- Supplier payments
- User withdrawals
- Disbursements
- Settlements
- Distribution of funds

The same payout resource can be used in all of these flows. The differences are usually in the corridor, beneficiary details, and rail.

### Core concepts

- `source currency`: the currency debited from the account balance
- `destination currency`: the currency received by the beneficiary
- `beneficiary`: the person or company receiving the funds
- `destination`: the destination market or country
- `rail`: the delivery method used to reach the beneficiary
- `remitter` or `sender`: the entity that initiates the transfer when the corridor requires it
- `externalId`: your business reference for the operation
- `payoutId`: the canonical identifier generated by the API
- `Idempotency-Key`: a value used to avoid duplicate submissions during retries

> The exact fields required for a payout depend on the country, currency, corridor, rail, beneficiary type, and compliance requirements.

---

## General payout flow

1. Ensure the account is pre-funded and has sufficient available balance.
2. Check available balance before creating the payout.
3. Optionally request an indicative quote if the corridor supports it.
4. Create the payout with the beneficiary, destination, and rail.
5. The API validates the request and begins processing.
6. A status update is sent through webhook.
7. You can retrieve the payout by ID as a fallback.

```text
1. Prefund account
   ↓
2. Check available balance
   ↓
3. Optionally request quote
   ↓
4. Create payout
   ↓
5. Payout enters processing
   ↓
6. Receive status update
   ↓
7. Payout completes or fails
```

---

## Lifecycle

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| `pending` | The payout was accepted and is waiting to be submitted or processed. |
| `processing` | The payout is being processed by the payout rail. |
| `completed` | The payout was delivered successfully. Terminal. |
| `failed` | The payout could not be completed. Terminal. |

> The lifecycle is asynchronous. A successful HTTP response means the payout resource was created. The final outcome may arrive later through the payout status update.

---

## Balance and funds

A payout is created against the available balance of the source currency. If the available balance is insufficient, the API returns an error.

| Event | Effect on balance |
|-------|-------------------|
| Payout is created | The requested amount is evaluated against available balance |
| Payout succeeds | Funds are considered delivered |
| Payout fails | The balance may be restored or made available again depending on the outcome |

A `402` response indicates insufficient balance for the requested payout.
